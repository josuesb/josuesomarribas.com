<p>Bitely is a modern, user-friendly recipe app designed to work seamlessly across multiple devices. This project explores how thoughtful UX research and a mobile-first approach can enhance the cooking experience for users.</p><h2>Understanding the User</h2><p>To create an intuitive and engaging experience, extensive research was conducted, including:</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Competitive analysis to identify gaps in existing recipe apps.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>User psychology insights to understand cooking habits and motivations.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>User interviews &amp; personas to define key pain points and expectations.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>User flows &amp; prototyping to ensure a smooth, frustration-free experience.</p></li></ul><h2>A Seamless Multi-Device Experience</h2><p>Cooking isn’t limited to just one screen—Bitely adapts to any device, allowing users to access their favorite recipes anywhere. With a clean, distraction-free design, the app keeps the focus on what matters most: the recipes.</p><p>A Modern Yet Simple Look</p><p>Bitely’s UI embraces:</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Minimalist typography for readability.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Bright, high-quality imagery to make recipes visually appealing.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>A focused color palette (White, Black, Red, and Pink) where red and pink highlight key actions in the user’s workflow.</p></li></ul><h2>Final Thoughts</h2><p>Bitely proves that good UX and responsive design can make everyday tasks—like cooking—more enjoyable and efficient. By prioritizing usability, accessibility, and a seamless experience across devices, this project showcases a thoughtful, research-driven approach to app design.</p><p>See the Full Case Study</p><p>Explore the complete project on Behance:</p><img alt="" src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/Gai2FowckGHmo6N9029z4O3o31U.png"><p><a href="https://www.behance.net/gallery/107840619/Bitely?utm_source=josuesb&amp;utm_medium=website&amp;utm_campaign=josuesb_home" target="_blank">Bitely on Behance</a></p>
