<h1>Introducing the Design Lab</h1><p>Welcome to the Design Lab, a space where I explore creative solutions to everyday design challenges. This section of my website is dedicated to bite-sized UI/UX experiments, where I tackle common problems and rethink solutions using limited time frames. </p><p><em>No, I’m not saying that sometimes "there is no time for design"…</em></p><img alt="" src="/images/framer-migrated/DouFAAasNMQcBdgUiI0CVX4qJrg.webp"><p>Now jokes aside, the goal is to push creativity, efficiency, and problem-solving skills, testing different tools and approaches along the way.</p><h2>The Challenge</h2><p>For this challenge, I’m creating a text-only landing page. The topic? My professional profile tailored for a UX position. The main objective is to design a page that helps recruiters quickly understand why I’m a great fit for their UX design roles.</p><p>I’ll be using Figma and Framer for this exercise. Figma will handle the research phase and low-to-mid-fidelity designs, while Framer will bring it all to life with a responsive, production-ready build.</p><h3>Competitor Analysis</h3><p>For this case, I conducted a quick research session—keeping in mind that these exercises are meant to be completed within a few hours. If you’re looking for a deeper dive, I have a more detailed <a href="/blog/conducting-a-competitor-analysis-for-a-travel-platform">Competitor Analysis</a> post that walks through the full process step by step.</p><p>I’ve divided the research into two parts: text-only landing pages and exceptional portfolios from other designers for inspiration.</p><img alt="" src="/images/framer-migrated/vbKXnHooQi4ouT5wvohwZds.webp"><p>I came across three portfolios from other designers.</p><p><a href="https://www.vilinskyy.com/" target="_blank">Alexander Vilinskyy</a> - Digital designer and founder of Super&nbsp;Clear </p><p>Very clean and direct, with a strong focus on clear messaging (which fits his “Super Clear” brand). The balance between typography and white space makes it easy to scan. Feels like a modern agency site.</p><img alt="" src="/images/framer-migrated/ahD3s68c3roMnPA1H7s4voCE2qU.webp"><p><a href="https://www.francescofagioli.me/" target="_blank">Francesco Fagioli</a>, A Senior Product Designer at SubbyX</p><p>&nbsp;A more personal, storytelling-driven approach. It has a friendly tone and showcases work with enough detail without being overwhelming. The project pages feel thoughtful and well-structured.</p><img alt="" src="/images/framer-migrated/nwkoSafZFikyPewSqoMDS9G18.webp"><p><a href="https://www.hansonwu.com/" target="_blank">Hanson Wu</a>, Principal Product Designer at Yahoo Sports</p><p>A mix of personality and professionalism. The layout is clean but has subtle touches that make it unique. Feels polished but not generic, which is hard to pull off.</p><img alt="" src="/images/framer-migrated/og17sqOwkKcmx0ZIwdhDJkDaEc.webp"><h2>Low Fidelity Wireframes</h2><p>For the Low-Fidelity Wireframes, I focused on structuring the content in a clear and digestible way, ensuring recruiters can quickly understand my profile. The wireframes prioritize hierarchy, readability, and key information placement without distractions. Since this is a text-only landing page, I experimented with different layouts to guide the reader’s attention effectively. At this stage, the goal is to establish a solid framework before refining the design further in higher fidelity stages.</p><img alt="" src="/images/framer-migrated/Fw5meG5WpAvf4mnE0dLtIdh7pno.webp"><h2>High Fidelity Wireframes</h2><p>For the High-Fidelity Wireframes, I am skipping the color and font selection since this page will be part of an existing website with established styles. Instead, I am focusing on refining the layout, typography hierarchy, and spacing to ensure a seamless integration with the current design system. The goal is to create a polished, structured page that maintains consistency while effectively communicating my professional profile to recruiters.</p><img alt="" src="/images/framer-migrated/OElCJZEt9qfehW1y4HbkVcfZHUc.webp"><h3>From Figma to Framer</h3><p>With the design structured in Figma, it’s time to bring it to life in Framer. This step is where the static wireframes evolve into a fully responsive, interactive landing page. Using Framer’s intuitive layout tools, I ensure that the design adapts seamlessly across different screen sizes while maintaining the intended hierarchy and readability.</p><p>Since this is a text-only landing page, I focus on precise spacing, typography, and responsiveness to create a smooth experience without relying on heavy visuals. The transition from Figma to Framer allows for real-time adjustments, ensuring that the final page feels just as polished in production as it does in the design phase.</p><p><a href="/building-solutions" target="_blank">Check out the result here</a></p><h2>Wrapping Up</h2><p>This Design Lab challenge pushed me to create a clear, structured, and effective text-only landing page within a limited time frame. From quick research and wireframing in Figma to bringing it to life in Framer, the goal was to ensure recruiters can quickly grasp why my profile is a strong fit for UX roles.</p><p>By focusing on content hierarchy, readability, and responsiveness, I crafted a page that aligns with an existing design system while delivering a seamless user experience. This exercise reinforces how even the simplest designs require thoughtful structure and execution.</p><p>Now, onto the next challenge!</p>
