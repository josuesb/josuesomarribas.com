<p>When designing a product, it's tempting to assume what users want. But creating designs on <a href="https://uxdesign.cc/ux-is-not-an-opinion-a9a32875d1ea" target="_blank">real data really makes the difference</a>. This is why to create something truly valuable, I needed real data. That's why <a href="/blog/how-user-research-shaped-my-travel-platform" target="_blank">I conducted a survey</a> to understand travelers' needs before designing my travel platform. In this article, I’ll explain how I used that research to create an accurate user persona.</p><h2>Step 1: Conducting the Survey</h2><p>To collect insights, I designed a survey using <a href="https://typeform.cello.so/0uvria0hyVD" target="_blank">Typeform</a> and asked travelers key questions about their habits, frustrations, and expectations. Some of the most important questions were:</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>How often do you travel?&nbsp;– To understand travel frequency.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>What type of trips do you prefer?&nbsp;– To identify different traveler profiles.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>How do you usually plan your trips?&nbsp;– To see what sources people trust.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>What’s missing in current travel platforms?&nbsp;– To discover gaps in the market.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>What frustrates you the most when planning trips?&nbsp;– To learn about common pain points.</p></li></ul><h2>Step 2: Analyzing the Data</h2><p>After collecting responses, I analyzed trends and patterns. Some key insights included:</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Most travelers take 3-4 trips per year.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Many prefer cultural and relaxation-based travel.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Hidden places are important, with an average interest score of 7.9/10.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Travelers rely on Google Maps, Instagram, and Booking.com for trip planning.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>The biggest frustrations include outdated travel information, a lack of hidden gems, and safety concerns.</p></li></ul><img alt="" src="https://framerusercontent.com/images/ldawO4M1LEo39GicZHCOXjGOqo.png"><h2>Step 3: Creating the User Persona</h2><p>Using this information, I developed&nbsp;Alex – The Hidden Gem Seeker, a persona that represents a key type of traveler who would benefit from my platform.</p><p>Alex – The Hidden Gem Seeker</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Age: 31</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Location: Mexico City</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Occupation: Freelance Graphic Designer</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Pronouns: They/Them</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Travel Style: Solo and Cultural Explorer</p></li></ul><p>Background &amp; Travel Habits</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Travels 3-4 times a year, often solo or in small groups.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Loves exploring beyond touristy areas and seeks unique, local experiences.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Uses Google Maps, Booking, and Instagram for trip planning.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Looks for budget-friendly stays but prioritizes safety and authenticity.</p></li></ul><p>Pain Points</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Struggles to find truly hidden places without spending hours researching.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Feels most travel guides are repetitive and lack local perspectives.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Worries about safety when traveling alone.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Finds queer-friendly travel information limited in many destinations.</p></li></ul><p>Needs &amp; Expectations</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>A reliable source for discovering underrated travel spots.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Real-time travel advice from locals and experienced travelers.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>A smart trip planner that helps them stay on budget.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>A community to connect with solo travelers and find inclusive spaces.</p></li></ul><h2>Final Thoughts</h2><p>By using real user research, I was able to develop a user persona that represents my target audience’s true needs. This process helped me design features that directly address common frustrations and improve the travel experience. Instead of guessing, I built a solution informed by real travelers, ensuring a stronger connection between my platform and its users.</p>
